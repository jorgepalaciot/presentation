About me

About Me

I'm an Industrial Engineer, graduated from Universidad Católica de Santa María (Arequipa, Peru), with more than nine years of independent experience in process consulting, administrative management and continuous improvement. My work combines two capabilities that rarely coexist in the same profile: the methodological lens of BPM and Lean to redesign processes, and the technical command of advanced Excel and Power BI to turn those processes into actionable data.

Between 2023 and 2024 I lived and worked in Australia and New Zealand, where I saw up close how larger-scale companies operate: management by metrics, role specialization and staff rotation. That international experience, combined with an advanced level of English (IELTS 6.5, British Council), lets me work just as comfortably with Spanish- or English-speaking teams and clients.

Today I combine independent consulting projects with the search for a role where I can bring that dual capability — process and data — on a permanent basis.

How I work — Methodology

  • Analysis: understanding the company in detail, including its structure, market and finances.
  • Diagnosis: identifying and prioritizing problems through analysis and stakeholder validation.
  • Planning: proposing solutions, developing projects and contingency plans.
  • Implementation: applying changes, training staff and adjusting along the way.
  • Verification: evaluating results against KPIs and, if needed, restarting the improvement cycle.

Every company has its own rhythm and challenges — that's why my approach is personalized and geared toward concrete results.
