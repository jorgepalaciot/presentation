01 — Hotel Process Consulting

Xima Hotels Chain

Multiple properties across the Xima Hotels chain needed to standardize operating processes between sites. I mapped processes and built characterization sheets per property, identifying operational friction points common across the chain — producing documented, standardizable processes and a foundation for future internal audits.

BPM · SIPOC · Process Mapping

02 — Process Management, Agro Sector

Agromercado / MIDAGRI

Agromercado (MIDAGRI) needed process management in a government/agricultural setting where staff weren't used to working this way. I ran awareness sessions, interviewed staff, mapped processes and implemented process management from scratch — characterizing critical processes and identifying bottlenecks to support the client's decision-making.

BPM · Staff Interviews · Bottleneck Detection

02.2025 – 05.2025 · Lima, Peru

03 — Process Improvement, Manufacturing

Isavela Home

Isavela Home, a manufacturing venture I founded, had a defect rate that was hurting customer satisfaction. I redesigned the production and quality-control process and managed production, logistics and e-commerce end to end — cutting production defects by 90% and directly improving customer satisfaction and retention.

Quality Control · Production · E-commerce

01.2022 – 03.2023 · Lima, Peru

Preventive Maintenance · Team Leadership
