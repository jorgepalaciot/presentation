Real Cases

Four verified projects, start to finish

Only cases I can document — no estimated numbers, no invented case studies. Each one is mapped the same way I map process work for clients: context, action, result.

Hotel Process Consulting — Xima Hotels Chain

Context: Multiple properties across the Xima Hotels chain needed to standardize operating processes between sites.

Action: Process mapping and characterization sheets per property, identifying operational friction points common across the chain.

Result: Documented, standardizable processes across sites — a foundation for future internal audits.

Process Management in the Agro Sector — Agromercado / MIDAGRI

Context: Consulting in a government/agricultural setting, with staff unfamiliar with process management.

Action: Raised awareness and implemented process management, staff interviews, process mapping and bottleneck detection.

Result: Critical processes characterized and bottlenecks identified to support the client's decision-making.

Production Defect Reduction — Isavela Home

Context: A self-founded manufacturing venture with a defect rate that was hurting customer satisfaction.

Action: Redesigned the production and quality-control process; managed production, logistics and e-commerce end to end.

Result: Cut production defects by 90%, directly improving customer satisfaction and retention.

Stakeholder Management & Preventive Maintenance — Edificio Esmeralda

Context: Managing a building that needed a structured maintenance program and a team to coordinate.

Action: Designed and implemented a preventive maintenance program; directly supervised a team of 5.

Result: 100% functionality guaranteed and a culture of proactive maintenance embedded in the team.
